SalaryWise – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026
Developer: Manish Saini
Email: mansaini111212@gmail.com

SalaryWise is a personal salary and financial management application that helps users manage their salary, expenses, bills, debts, reminders, and related financial information.

This Privacy Policy explains how SalaryWise handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Store

SalaryWise stores information that you provide while using the application.

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Account/profile type

Financial Information

  • Salary or income information
  • Expense information
  • Debt information
  • Bills and payment information
  • Budget information
  • Other financial information entered by you

Security Information

  • A 4-digit PIN used to access your local SalaryWise profile

Images and Files

  • Bill photographs
  • Bill images
  • Other files or images that you choose to attach to your financial records

Application Settings

  • Language preferences
  • Theme preferences
  • Other application settings

2. How Your Information Is Stored

SalaryWise is designed as a local-first application.

The information listed above is stored locally on your Android device using local application storage.

SalaryWise does not operate a custom backend server for storing your personal or financial information.

Your salary, expenses, bills, debts, PIN, profile information, and locally stored images are not uploaded to a SalaryWise cloud server.

3. Camera and Bill Images

SalaryWise may request access to your device's camera to allow you to take photographs of bills or other documents for use within the application.

Images selected or captured for use in SalaryWise are stored locally on your device.

You can manage camera permission through your Android device settings.

4. Advertising

SalaryWise displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect or process certain information for advertising purposes, which may include:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Advertising-related information

The information processed by AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

SalaryWise does not intentionally send your salary, expenses, debts, bills, PIN, or locally stored financial records to AdMob.

For more information about Google's advertising practices, please visit:

Google Advertising Privacy Information

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information.

Your locally stored personal and financial information is not intentionally shared with third parties by SalaryWise.

Google AdMob may independently process device and advertising-related information for advertising purposes as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

SalaryWise is designed to keep your personal and financial information stored locally on your device.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect your device using appropriate security measures such as a screen lock, PIN, password, or biometric authentication.

7. Local PIN

SalaryWise uses a 4-digit PIN to provide local access to your SalaryWise profile.

The PIN functionality is handled locally within the application and does not require an online account or remote authentication service.

8. Data Deletion

SalaryWise provides a Delete Account option within the application.

When you use the Delete Account feature, the locally stored SalaryWise information associated with your account/profile is deleted, including applicable:

  • Profile information
  • Salary information
  • Expense records
  • Debt records
  • Bill records
  • Financial information
  • Application preferences
  • Locally stored images and files

You can also remove SalaryWise and its locally stored application data by uninstalling the application or clearing its application data through Android settings, subject to standard Android behavior and any device backup functionality.

9. Data Retention

SalaryWise does not maintain a remote copy of your personal or financial records.

Your information remains stored locally on your device until you delete it through the available deletion functionality, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Information processed by third-party advertising services may be retained according to those services' respective privacy policies.

10. Information We Do Not Collect

SalaryWise does not require users to provide:

  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Physical address
  • Online account credentials
  • OTP information

SalaryWise does not maintain your financial information on a custom cloud server.

11. Third-Party Services

SalaryWise uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google may process certain device and advertising-related information as part of providing its advertising services.

12. Children's Privacy

SalaryWise is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us at the email address below.

13. Your Choices

  • Manage application permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage advertising-related settings through your device or Google account where available.
  • Delete your locally stored SalaryWise information using the Delete Account feature.
  • Clear the application's data through Android settings.
  • Uninstall SalaryWise from your device.

Some application features may not function if the required permission is disabled.
